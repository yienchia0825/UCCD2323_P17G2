About Animation and Multinedia Club

Creativity, technology and multimedia brought together.

Who We Are

Animation & Multimedia Club for students who are interested in animation, video editing, digital art and multimedia production.

The club provides opportunities for members to develop creative and technical skills through projects, workshops, competitions and collaborative activities.

We believe multimedia is a powerful way to communicate ideas, tell stories and express creativity.

🎬

Our Passion

Bringing creative ideas to life through animation, video and digital media.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To provide a creative environment where students can improve their animation and multimedia skills, collaborate on projects and share their ideas.

🚀 Our Vision

To build an active and creative multimedia community where students can explore new technologies and produce meaningful digital content.

What Members Can Gain

  • Improve animation and digital art skills.
  • Learn video editing and multimedia production.
  • Participate in creative projects and workshops.
  • Develop teamwork and communication skills.
  • Share creative ideas with other students.
  • Showcase multimedia projects and achievements.
